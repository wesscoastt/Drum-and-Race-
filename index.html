HOW TO PLAY

STEER with the ◄ ► buttons, or hold the left / right side of the road. Keyboard: AD or arrows.

NITRO — hold it to boost. While boosting you SMASH through traffic for big points. Nitro tank drains; grab N2O cans or refill in the garage.

NEAR MISS — pass a car close without touching it. Near misses, drums and cash build your COMBO. Every 3 in a row raises the multiplier.

DRUMS = points + a little cash. CASH STACKS = money. WRENCHES = fresh tread.

TIRES wear down as you drive. Bald tires slide in the corners. Wet tracks are slick.

WATCH THE BLINKERS — cars signal before they change lanes. Cops weave on purpose from level 3 up.

LEVELS come from distance. Every level adds speed and traffic. Spend your cash in the GARAGE on new cars and upgrades.

SAVE from the pause menu to keep your exact spot in a race and come back later.
